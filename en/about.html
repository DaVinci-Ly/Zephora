About Zephora

A Libyan company in Benghazi that imports raw materials for the chemical industries and supplies them to factories and distributors.

Who we are

Three materials we know well, instead of a long list

Our full name is “Zephora for the Import of Raw Materials and Chemicals”. We work from Benghazi and import the materials that detergent manufacturing and disinfection in Libya are built on.

We chose to start with just three materials: SLES, LABSA and sodium hypochlorite. This focus lets us know each material well, follow its quality from one shipment to the next, and answer your questions accurately.

  • Clear origin

    You know where each material comes from before you buy.

  • Written specifications

    We give you the specifications of the shipment in stock, not general promises.

  • Direct contact

    You talk to the company itself, with no middlemen.

  • Formal dealing

    A registered company, and every sale comes with an invoice.

Origin

Where our materials come from

For each material we chose the country that suits it best, for quality and proximity.

China

SLES 70%

One of the world's largest producers of detergent materials, with high output and steady quality.

Saudi Arabia

LABSA 96%

Made from petroleum derivatives, and the Saudi petrochemical industry is close to the source of its raw material.

Egypt

NaOCl 12%

Close to Libya, which matters for a material that loses strength the longer it is shipped and stored.
