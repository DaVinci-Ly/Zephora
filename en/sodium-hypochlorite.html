Sodium Hypochlorite

Known as “liquid chlorine”

A strong chlorine solution that kills germs and bacteria. It is used in water treatment, disinfection and industrial cleaning, and household bleach is made from it after dilution.

Concentration
12%
Origin
Egypt
Form
Yellow-green liquid

What is it?

Sodium hypochlorite is the active ingredient in the bleach everyone knows. The difference is that household bleach is diluted to about 5%, while this solution is 12%.

That is why it is used as it is in factories and water plants, or diluted with water to make and bottle household bleach.

Where is it used?

  • Water treatmentDrinking water, wells and tanks.
  • Pool disinfectionKeeping pool water clean and safe.
  • Making household bleachDiluted, then bottled for sale.
  • Sanitary disinfectionHospitals, food factories and kitchens.
  • Laundries and textilesWhitening fabrics and removing stains.
  • Industrial cleaningDisinfecting floors, surfaces and equipment.

Typical specifications

AppearanceClear liquid, yellow-green
Concentration12%
pH11 – 13
DensityAbout 1.2 g/cm³
CAS number7681-52-9

The concentration drops gradually with time and heat, so we tell you the concentration of the shipment in stock when you buy.

Storage and safety

Never mix it with acids or ammonia cleaners
  • Mixing releases toxic chlorine gas, even with ordinary household cleaners.
  • Store in opaque, closed plastic containers in a cool place away from the sun.
  • It is corrosive to skin and eyes and ruins clothes: use gloves and safety goggles.
  • Use it within a few months of receiving it so it keeps its strength.

To make household bleach of about 5%: mix 4 parts of the solution with 6 parts of water, and add the solution to the water, not the other way round.
