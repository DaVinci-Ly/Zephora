Terms of use

Short points explaining what the site offers and what it does not.

Purpose of the site

The site introduces Zephora and the materials we import. Sales and agreements on prices and quantities are made directly with us through our official channels.

Prices and availability

Prices change with each shipment and with the exchange rate, so we give you the price when you ask. Available quantities also change from time to time.

Specifications

The specifications published on the site are typical values to introduce each material. The binding specifications are those written for each shipment, which we provide on request.

Safety

The materials we import are concentrated chemicals. What we publish about storage and use is general guidance, and does not replace the safety instructions of each material or the experience of specialists.

Official channels

Our channels are only those listed on the contact page. We are not responsible for any dealing carried out through other numbers or accounts.

Logo and name

The company logo and trade name belong to the company, and may not be used in a way that suggests a relationship or authorisation that does not exist.
