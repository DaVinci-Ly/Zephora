SLES 70%

Sodium Lauryl Ether Sulfate

Known in the market as “Texapon”

The material that makes foam in shampoo, liquid soap and dishwashing liquid. A thick paste that is diluted with water during manufacturing.

  • Shampoo & shower gel
  • Liquid soap
  • Dishwashing liquid
  • Liquid detergents

LABSA 96%

Linear Alkylbenzene Sulfonic Acid

Known in the market as “sulfonic acid”

The main cleaning agent in washing powder, dishwashing liquid and floor cleaners. A thick brown liquid of high concentration, neutralised with caustic soda during manufacturing.

  • Washing powder
  • Dishwashing liquid
  • Floor cleaners
  • Industrial cleaners
Concentration
96%
Origin
Saudi Arabia
Form
Viscous brown liquid

Sodium Hypochlorite

A strong chlorine solution for disinfecting water and surfaces. Household bleach is made from it after dilution.
